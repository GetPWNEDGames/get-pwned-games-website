IN THE WORKSHOP // GAME 001

MEET SORT & SPROCKET.

IN DEVELOPMENT

An old workshop. A pile of broken machines. One puzzle at a time, bring it all back to life.

Sort & Sprocket is our first original mechanical puzzle game. Organize gears with distinctive shapes, bring machines back online, route parts through an industrial workshop, and build working gear trains using tooth counts and rotation.

It's a game about figuring things out—and enjoying the moment when a machine finally works because you made it work. And if something breaks again, Sort and Sprocket might have a few ideas.

WORKSHOP PERSONNEL // 002

MEET SORT & SPROCKET.

TWO RESIDENTS / ZERO RENT

The game is named for the two unlikely workshop partners who bring it to life. Sort is our cheese-powered problem solver. Sprocket is our orange chaos engineer. Inspired by the real Obi and Rocket—two unlikely roommates with very different definitions of helping.
